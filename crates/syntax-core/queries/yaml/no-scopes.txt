# and `null`, which the grammar parses as `boolean_scalar` and `null_scalar`
# and which highlights.scm captures as `@boolean` and `@constant.builtin` —
# what they actually are. Neither resolves to `keyword` (they are not under
# it in the scope hierarchy), and capturing them as `@keyword` just to
# satisfy this harness would put a lie in the taxonomy every theme reads.
